The Pokédex

POKéDEX on the start menu opens BW2’s Pokédex, with its screens and controls, on Platinum’s data: the Sinnoh Pokédex (and the National one once you have it), with Platinum’s numbers, categories, heights, weights and entries. The Pokémon are always BW2’s sprites. It’s always on.

It’s in BW2’s colours, with its grids and stripes turned into solid fills.

Buttons

Section titled “Buttons”
ButtonWhat it does
XCloses the Pokédex and the start menu, back to the field, as in BW2
BBack to the start menu
STARTSearch
SELECTThe Habitat List’s area list, once you have it
YNothing: BW2’s Y check boxes are hidden, as Platinum’s Y can’t open a Pokédex page

The Pokédex reopens on the species and list you left.

The list and search

Section titled “The list and search”

The list shows the Sinnoh Pokédex, or the National one once you have it, with BW2’s SEEN and OBTAINED counters on Platinum’s counts. Before the National Pokédex, a species from outside Sinnoh shows ”???” as its number.

Search finds seen Pokémon by name, type, colour or shape, and sorts them by number, name, weight or height.

A Pokémon’s pages

Section titled “A Pokémon’s pages”
PageWhat it shows
InfoPlatinum’s number, category, height, weight and entry, with BW2’s sprite
AreaWhere it lives on a map of Sinnoh (below)
CryPlays Platinum’s cry and draws its waveform
FormsIts forms, sexes and shiny colours that you’ve seen (below)

Platinum’s size comparison and foreign-language entries are gone.

The area page

Section titled “The area page”

A map of Sinnoh with the places the Pokémon lives pulsing, as in Platinum.

  • The time selector at the bottom right (or L and R) switches between morning, day and night. It starts at the current time.
  • A place lit normally means you can meet the Pokémon there as usual. A place in Platinum’s special look means it’s only there through a phenomenon or one of Platinum’s special ways: honey trees, the Great Marsh’s daily Pokémon, Feebas’s tiles, and (after the National Pokédex) the Trophy Garden and the Poké Radar.
  • Phenomena show at every time of day.
  • Touch a place for its name and kinds of habitat. With the Habitat List, touch it again to open that area’s list.

The forms page

Section titled “The forms page”

It shows up once Platinum’s forms upgrade is in your Pokédex: the scientist in the gate between Route 218 and Canalave City gives it. It lists each form or sex you’ve seen, for the Pokémon that have them, and a shiny view.

Note

Shinies count from the version that added the Pokédex (0.2.0) on: shinies you saw before that aren’t recorded.

After a catch

Section titled “After a catch”

When you catch a Pokémon for the first time, BW2’s registration screen shows its entry, with Platinum’s Pokédex data (the Sinnoh number, or the National one once you have it, and its category, height, weight and entry). A Pokémon you’d caught before shows on the Pokédex’s cover instead, with its cry.

Then comes the nickname question, with Platinum’s YES and NO on the touch screen, Platinum’s naming screen, and Platinum’s box message if your party is full.

A small Poké Ball on a wild Pokémon’s HP bar means you’ve already caught its species.
