Battle backgrounds

Battles use BW2’s backgrounds, picked for where you’re standing in Sinnoh.

Routes

Section titled “Routes”

Routes, and the catching tutorial, use BW2’s open-field look (its Route 19). The platforms under the Pokémon change with the ground you’re standing on when the battle starts:

Standing onPlatforms
GrassA lawn
A pathBare ground
Tall grassA ring of tall grass
Rock in the open (not in snowfields)BW2’s mountain background, with rocky ground
A bridgeBW2’s bridges’ look (below)
Route 206’s Cycling Road deckGrey concrete

Towns and most rooms keep the backgrounds they already had. Gyms and the Elite Four’s rooms have their own.

Bridges

Section titled “Bridges”

A battle on a route’s bridge (Routes 205, 207 to 212, 215, 216, 218, 225, 227 and 228, Iron Island and Sendoff Spring) looks like BW2’s bridges: its town background, with floor platforms, as the bridges in Canalave City, Sunyshore City and the Resort Area already did. Route 216’s is in winter, like the snowfields’ battles.

  • Route 206’s Cycling Road keeps its grey concrete.
  • Under a bridge, the ground you stand on picks the look, as anywhere on a route.
  • Bridges in caves keep the cave’s look, and a Pokémon fished up from a bridge is battled on the water.

Seasons and time of day

Section titled “Seasons and time of day”

The field is BW2’s in summer, its cooler green, everywhere except snowfields, which are BW2’s route in winter. The light follows Platinum’s times of day.

Caves

Section titled “Caves”

Caves use BW2’s dark slate cave with rock platforms. Surfing in a cave stays in the cave, with water under the Pokémon, instead of Platinum’s open sea.

Gyms

Section titled “Gyms”

Every Gym has a background and platforms of its own, picked to fit how the Gym looks, in every room of it where you battle:

GymThe GymIts battles
Oreburgh (Rock)A quarry walled in rockBW2’s brown cave (Twist Mountain’s), on rocky earth
Eterna (Grass)A garden under a painted skyThe routes’ open field, on lawns
Veilstone (Fighting)A training hall’s wooden floorBW2’s own Gyms’ background, its warm floor, on pale floor platforms
Pastoria (Water)Walkways in a poolWater to the horizon, on pale walkways
Hearthome (Ghost)Dark rooms full of eyesShauntal’s room, the violet arena of BW2’s Ghost-type Elite Four
Canalave (Steel)Steel walls and girdersDark iron octagons under a grey sky
Snowpoint (Ice)An ice rinkKyurem’s chamber, with ice spikes around the platforms
Sunyshore (Electric)Grey machinery, yellow panelsMarshal’s room, grey, its platforms ringed in yellow

Gyms are indoors, so their battles are lit as at midday at any hour, Eterna’s and Pastoria’s open-air backgrounds too. Only the picture changes: moves that depend on where you battle, like Nature Power and Secret Power, work as they do in Platinum’s Gyms.

The Elite Four and Cynthia

Section titled “The Elite Four and Cynthia”

Each of the Elite Four battles in one of BW2’s Elite Four rooms, recoloured to Platinum’s background in that room: dark, with bright streaks.

RoomBackgroundColours
Aaron’sGrimsley’s roomDark emerald to mint
Bertha’sN’s throne roomDark brown, with gold streaks
Flint’sMarshal’s roomMaroon to crimson
Lucian’sShauntal’s roomViolet to magenta
Cynthia’sBW2’s Champion background (Iris’s)As in BW2

The platforms in all five rooms are drawn like Platinum’s: a pale disc with a thin ring inside, a band at the edge and a dark outline, and two lines at the sides joining the ring to the band, flat, in Platinum’s colours for each room.

The Distortion World

Section titled “The Distortion World”

The Distortion World has a field of its own: BW2’s Champion background without its stars and coloured streaks, in the Distortion World’s colours, its floor’s swirl still turning, on dusky rose platforms like Platinum’s. Giratina has no platform under it, as in Platinum. Cynthia keeps BW2’s Champion background as it is.

Safari Zone and Pal Park

Section titled “Safari Zone and Pal Park”

In the Great Marsh and Pal Park, your trainer isn’t shown once the battle starts (as in BW2), and there’s no platform on your side, where no Pokémon stands. See Battle screens.
