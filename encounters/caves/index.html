Caves and dungeons

What each cave’s phenomena give: dust clouds on its floor (when they don’t hold an item) and rippling water on its pools.

Note

Chances are per phenomenon: when you step into one, this is how likely each Pokémon is. Levels are a little above the area’s usual wild Pokémon. A honey tree Pokémon is whatever a honey tree in the area would give you: see Honey trees.
