Towns, lakes and other places

What the phenomena give everywhere that isn’t a route or a cave: the lakes, Eterna Forest, the towns you can surf or fish in, and the like.

Note

Chances are per phenomenon: when you step into one, this is how likely each Pokémon is. Levels are a little above the area’s usual wild Pokémon. A honey tree Pokémon is whatever a honey tree in the area would give you: see Honey trees.

Eterna Forest

Section titled “Eterna Forest”

Tip

Murkrow and Misdreavus, Diamond’s and Pearl’s, live in the forest’s rustling grass. Rotom comes only from rustling grass in the clearing by the Old Chateau, past the Cut trees: anywhere else, its slot gives one of the forest’s other Pokémon. Leafeon is the forest’s, home of the Moss Rock.
